# Lab 12 — TCP/IP + OSI Troubleshooting Challenge
## Scenario
A workstation can reach some services but not others.

## Tasks
Work through faults at each layer:
- Physical link
- Ethernet/VLAN
- IP addressing
- ARP
- Routing
- DNS
- TCP/UDP ports
- TLS/SSL/application layer

Use ping, tracert/traceroute, ipconfig/ifconfig, arp, nslookup/dig, Test-NetConnection/netcat and packet captures where appropriate.

## Deliverables
- OSI-to-command mapping
- Evidence
- Root cause
