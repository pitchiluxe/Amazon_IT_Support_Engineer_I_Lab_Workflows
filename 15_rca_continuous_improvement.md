# Lab 15 — Network Service Outage & Root Cause Analysis
## Scenario
A recurring outage happens every few days.

## Tasks
- Gather logs and timestamps.
- Identify patterns.
- Build a 5-Whys analysis.
- Separate symptoms from root cause.
- Recommend a permanent fix.
- Quantify operational impact.
- Create a change plan and rollback plan.

## Deliverables
- RCA document
- 5-Whys
- Corrective action plan
- Change record
