# Lab 11 — Cisco Switching & VLAN Troubleshooting
## Scenario
A new operations area is being segmented into VLANs.

## Tasks
- Build a small topology in Cisco Packet Tracer or on authorized hardware.
- Configure access and trunk ports.
- Create multiple VLANs.
- Configure inter-VLAN routing in the simulator.
- Verify with show commands.
- Document port assignments.

## Break/Fix
Introduce a wrong VLAN, trunk mismatch, shutdown port, or incorrect gateway and isolate the fault.

## Deliverables
- Topology
- VLAN/port table
- Configuration backup
- Troubleshooting report
