# 12-Week Job Preparation Roadmap

## Weeks 1–2: Foundations
Labs 01–05
Focus: Windows Server, AD, DNS, DHCP, permissions.

## Weeks 3–4: File/Print/Endpoint
Labs 06–09
Focus: DFS, Print Services, endpoint deployment, PC repair.

## Weeks 5–6: Multi-OS + Networking
Labs 10–13
Focus: Windows/Linux/macOS, Cisco, TCP/IP, OSI, cabling.

## Weeks 7–8: Operations Reliability
Labs 14–15
Focus: high availability, incidents, RCA and continuous improvement.

## Week 9: Collaboration + Infrastructure
Labs 16–18
Focus: conference rooms, assets, IT closets/data-center fundamentals.

## Week 10: Projects + Documentation
Labs 19–20
Focus: project management, SOPs, change/rollback.

## Week 11: Shift Simulation
Lab 21
Run multiple incidents under time pressure.

## Week 12: Capstone + Interview
Lab 22
Complete the warehouse IT environment and practice explaining every design decision.

## Weekly routine
- 3 days: hands-on lab work
- 1 day: troubleshooting without instructions
- 1 day: documentation/SOP
- 1 day: interview questions
- 1 day: review/rest

## Success standard
Do not simply follow a tutorial. For each lab, intentionally break one component, diagnose it from evidence, restore service, and document the root cause.
