# Lab 18 — IT Closet / Mini Data Center Build
## Scenario
You are preparing a small network/server room.

## Tasks
- Design rack layout.
- Document power and UPS considerations.
- Plan switch/server placement.
- Label patch panels.
- Create environmental monitoring checklist.
- Document cable management and access controls.
- Build a logical topology.

## Deliverables
- Rack elevation
- Power/UPS plan
- Cable map
- Maintenance checklist
- Risk register
