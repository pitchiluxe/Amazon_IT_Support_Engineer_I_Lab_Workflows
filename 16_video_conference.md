# Lab 16 — Video Conference Room Support
## Scenario
A management meeting begins in 10 minutes and the room system has no audio.

## Tasks
- Troubleshoot display, camera, microphone, speakers and network.
- Verify correct input/output devices.
- Check cable/adapter issues.
- Validate application permissions.
- Perform a complete pre-meeting test.

## Break/Fix
Create an incorrect audio device selection or disconnected peripheral.

## Deliverables
- Room checklist
- Fault tree
- Incident report
