# Lab 17 — IT Asset Deployment, Return & Liquidation
## Scenario
20 employee devices are being refreshed.

## Tasks
- Create asset records.
- Assign equipment.
- Perform deployment acceptance.
- Process returned devices.
- Separate repair, redeploy, recycle and liquidation candidates.
- Securely remove organizational data according to your organization's approved policy.

## Deliverables
- Asset lifecycle workflow
- Chain-of-custody form
- Deployment checklist
- Return/liquidation checklist
