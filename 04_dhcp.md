# Lab 04 — DHCP Scope & Failure Recovery
## Scenario
New scanners/workstations are receiving incorrect or no IP addresses.

## Tasks
- Create a DHCP scope.
- Configure exclusions/reservations.
- Configure gateway/DNS options.
- Verify leases.
- Test client renewal.

## Break/Fix
Simulate an exhausted scope, incorrect option, or client-side DHCP failure.

## Deliverables
- Scope design
- Lease evidence
- Troubleshooting decision tree
- Recovery procedure
