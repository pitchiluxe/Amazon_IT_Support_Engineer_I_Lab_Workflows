# Lab 14 — High-Availability Operations Incident
## Scenario
A critical service becomes unavailable during a simulated production window.

## Tasks
- Define service impact.
- Establish incident timeline.
- Check monitoring and dependencies.
- Identify whether the issue is client, network, DNS, server, authentication, storage or application.
- Restore service using the safest reversible action.
- Validate production readiness.
- Conduct post-incident review.

## Break/Fix
Use a controlled outage such as stopping a non-production service or disconnecting a test VM from its network.

## Deliverables
- Incident ticket
- Timeline
- Communications log
- Root cause
- Corrective actions
- Preventive actions
